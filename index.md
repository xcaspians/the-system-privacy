# The System: SideQuests — Privacy

*Last updated: October 25, 2026*

The System: SideQuests is made by Jacksen Edmond, an independent developer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own. What you enter stays on your iPhone (and in your iPhone’s own backups, if you turn them on), unless you choose to send it to an AI as described below. The developer never receives your Goals, Journal, photos or chats.

## AI features

If you add an Anthropic API key, it’s stored in the iOS Keychain and sent only to Anthropic’s API, only when you use an AI feature, and only after you allow it (“Share with Anthropic?”). Requests are made under your own Anthropic account, so Anthropic’s Commercial Terms (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms) apply to them. Anthropic explains how long it keeps API data here: https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data

A request carries what that feature needs. A new Goal sends your answers, rest days and daily requirement. Changing or extending a Goal sends its plan and which quests you’ve done. A chat sends your messages plus the context shown above the chat (by default: the plan outline, where you are in it, your level and streak, any active penalty, and your rest days). Your Journal is sent only if you switch it on for that chat. Photos are sent only when you attach one to a chat message. Your name, your other Goals and your reminders are never sent.

To stop sending anything to Anthropic, remove your key in Settings. Chats you have with your key are saved on your device (the 50 most recent) until you delete them, and are never included in exports.

Copy-paste mode sends nothing from the app itself. “Copy” puts the prompt on your clipboard. “Copy & open in Claude” (or ChatGPT) also opens that site with the prompt inside the link when it fits, so the site receives the prompt as soon as it opens, even before you press send, and it may be kept in your browser history. That site’s own privacy policy applies to whatever it receives.

## Your files

Photos are resized and re-saved without their location and camera data. They stay on the device unless you attach one to a chat message or include them in an export.

An export is a file with your whole save: Goals, progress, Journal, reminders and a few settings, plus your photos if you choose “with photos”. It never includes your API key or chats. A shared Goal contains only its plan, with no progress. You choose where either one goes.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is pushed from a server.

## What the developer receives

The app sends nothing to the developer. During beta testing, Apple’s TestFlight collects crash reports and usage data, and any feedback or screenshots you send, and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/). If you were invited by email, the developer can see that email address. If you joined through a public link, Apple doesn’t show your name or email to the developer. The developer uses this only to fix and improve the app, and doesn’t share it with anyone. If you email the developer, your email is kept only as long as needed to reply. Ask at the address below to have it, or your tester access, deleted.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data erases your save, Journal, photos, chats, reminders, settings and your API key. Deleting the app removes the same things. iOS can keep the API key in the Keychain after the app is deleted, but if you install the app again, it deletes that key the first time it opens.

## Children

The System isn’t directed to children under 13. Anthropic’s API is for adults, so the AI features require an adult’s own API key.
